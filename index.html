Frequently asked questions

Important information for the current preview environment.

Can I deposit money right now?

No. Deposits, withdrawals and client-money custody are intentionally disabled in this preview environment.

Is live trading available?

No. The market values shown are illustrative. A production execution connection has not been activated.

Can I create an account?

Yes. You can create a development client account and access the client dashboard.

Will Virexa have an admin portal?

Yes. The full-stack development build is intended to include administrator authentication, client management, KYC workflow and audit visibility.
