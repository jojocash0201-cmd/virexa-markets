Frequently Asked Questions

Important information about this concept environment.

Is this real-money trading?

No. Virexa is currently a simulated trading environment. Real-money trading is not enabled.

Can I deposit money?

No. Deposits, withdrawals and client-money custody are not enabled in this version.

Are the market prices real?

The prices displayed in this demo are illustrative simulated market data.

What happens after the demo?

Any future production launch would require appropriate legal, regulatory, KYC/AML and operational arrangements.
