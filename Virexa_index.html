Frequently asked questions

Important information about this development environment.

Is Virexa Markets live?

No. This is a development and simulation environment. Live trading and client-money services are not enabled.

Can I create an account?

Yes. The registration form connects to the Virexa development backend and creates a development client account.

Are the market prices real?

No. The figures displayed on this page are illustrative and should not be treated as live market quotes.

Can I deposit or withdraw money?

No. Deposit and withdrawal functionality is deliberately disabled in the current development backend.

What happens after registration?

You are signed into the development client dashboard, where your account profile and development balance/equity can be displayed.
